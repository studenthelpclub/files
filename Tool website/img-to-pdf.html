Guide: How to Convert Image to PDF

Welcome to Student Help Club. Our converter is designed for students who need to merge multiple photos into a single PDF document for online applications and exam forms.

1. Simple Multi-Selection

Click on "Add Images" to pick JPG or PNG files. You can select all your documents at once from your mobile gallery or PC storage.

2. Customize Your Layout

Once uploaded, use the Page Orientation setting to choose between Portrait (vertical) or Landscape (horizontal) view. You can also reorder images easily.

3. Privacy First Policy

Your documents are processed locally in your browser. We do not upload your sensitive files to any server, making this tool 100% secure for private use.

Quick Steps:

  • Select images from your device.
  • Arrange the order or remove images.
  • Choose orientation and click "Create PDF Now".
  • Download your high-quality PDF file.
